Who we are

An Omani initiative advancing space awareness and scientific ambition.

OSI bridges the gap between Omani students and the country’s growing space sector. The initiative challenges the idea that space-related careers are abstract, distant, or inaccessible by creating practical routes into knowledge, mentorship, and applied experience.

What we do

Programs designed to move students from curiosity to capability.

Awareness Campaigns

Mentorship & Guidance

Structured support connecting students with role models, academic pathways, and practical next steps.

Institutional Training

Custom sessions for schools, youth organizations, and partners seeking stronger STEM and space-sector engagement.

Why it matters

Oman needs more students who can see themselves inside the space economy.

Space is not only about astronauts. It includes engineering, software, data, policy, communications, design, entrepreneurship, and scientific research. OSI’s role is to make those pathways legible and actionable.
