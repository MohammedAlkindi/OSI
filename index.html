Who we are

An Omani initiative turning space ambition into practical direction.

OSI bridges the gap between students and Oman’s growing space ecosystem. We make the field easier to understand, easier to enter, and more exciting to build for.

What we do

Programs designed to move students from curiosity to capability.

Student Workshops

Interactive sessions covering space science, aerospace pathways, satellite systems, research exposure, and STEM foundations.

Awareness Campaigns

Public-facing educational content that makes space careers, institutions, and national opportunities easier to understand.

Mentorship & Guidance

Structured support connecting students with role models, academic pathways, and practical next steps.

Institutional Training

Why it matters

Oman needs more students who can see themselves inside the space economy.

Space is not only about astronauts. It includes engineering, software, data, policy, communications, design, entrepreneurship, and scientific research. OSI’s role is to make those pathways legible and actionable.
